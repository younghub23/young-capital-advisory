§ IV

The Framework

Every mandate passes through the same four movements. They are deliberately slow. They compound.

  1. i.

    Observe

    We begin with the primary sources — leases, registries, transport flows, planning filings. No vendor dashboard substitutes for the document.

  2. ii.

    Model

    We build small, inspectable models. A committee should be able to read the code, not only the summary. We publish methodology with every figure.

  3. iii.

    Contest

    Every conclusion is argued against its strongest counter-case before it leaves the studio. Our work product has been tested; it does not merely confirm.

  4. iv.

    Advise

    Finally, we tell you what we would do. Plainly, in writing, in a document the chief executive can finish on a single train journey.
