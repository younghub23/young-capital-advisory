Model Review & Audit

Independent third-party validation of financial models for investment committees, capital partners, and due diligence processes. We identify errors before they become costly mistakes.

What We Check

  • Formula accuracy and circular reference detection
  • Cash flow timing and discounting methodology
  • Waterfall logic and promote calculations
  • Debt sizing and covenant compliance
  • Tax calculations and depreciation schedules
  • Sensitivity and scenario consistency

Deliverables

Audit Report Detailed findings document with severity ratings and recommended fixes
Error Log Itemized list of issues with cell references and correction guidance
Sign-off Letter Professional attestation suitable for investment committee presentation
